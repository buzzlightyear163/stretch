anon@stretch ~ % man stretch

the fine print.

What's live, what's beta, where the data comes from, and what it will never ask you for.

is this financial advice?

No. STRETCH shows what is happening on-chain and how its checks read it. It never tells you to buy, and a CLEAR coin can still dump after migration. That's why the log shows the after.

what's live and what's beta?

Live: the board, the six checks, the coin checklists and the migration log, read from public data every few seconds. Beta: the engine's buys and sells. For now they run by hand from one public wallet, so you can verify every trade.

where does the data come from?

Jupiter's public "about to graduate" feed for curve progress, holders, dev and top-10 share and 5-minute flow on pump.fun and Raydium LaunchLab (incl. bonk.fun). DexScreener for how a coin trades after migration. PumpPortal's free stream for migrations as they land. No API keys.

how is a coin graded?

Six checks: holders growing, dev hasn't sold, no bundle selling out, top 10 under 30%, more buys than sells in the last 5 minutes, and real unique buyers (8+ wallets in 5 minutes). All pass: CLEAR. One miss: WATCH. Two misses, or a dev or bundle caught selling while STRETCH watches: FAIL.

what can't it do?

It can't see the future. "Dev holds 0%" can also mean the dev never bought. A dev or bundle selling is caught from the moment STRETCH starts watching a coin, not before. The hit rate counts what the engine would have done, from the moment the log started.

how does the holder gate work?

Connect a wallet and STRETCH reads its $STRETCH balance on-chain. Holders see every CLEAR call the second it fires. Everyone else sees it a few seconds later. It never asks you to sign anything. Never paste a seed phrase or private key anywhere.

will stretch DM me?

Never. Anyone who does is not us.
